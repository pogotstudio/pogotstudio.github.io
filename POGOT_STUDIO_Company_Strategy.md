# POGOT STUDIO — Compact Company Strategy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Business model:** B2B product and technology studio  
**Primary focus:** Embedded systems, IoT, AIoT, and connected physical products

---

## 1. Company Positioning

> **POGOT STUDIO helps companies turn technology ideas into commercially and technically viable products.**

POGOT STUDIO operates between business, product, and engineering. The company helps customers determine **what should be built**, **how it should be built**, and, when required, **builds the product itself**.

The company is not positioned as a generic software house or a generic IoT vendor. Its specialization is the engineering of **connected physical products** where hardware, firmware, connectivity, edge systems, cloud software, and AI/AIoT may need to work together.

---

## 2. Core Customer Problem

Many companies want to adopt embedded, IoT, AI, or AIoT technology because the market is moving toward these technologies. However, they may not yet know:

- whether the technology is actually appropriate for their business;
- what product should be built;
- what the minimum viable solution looks like;
- how much development and production will cost;
- what architecture should be used;
- whether their existing prototype is technically sound;
- how to move from prototype to a real product.

POGOT STUDIO solves this uncertainty and execution gap.

### Core problem statement

> **Companies know they need technology, but often lack the product and engineering capability to determine the right solution and turn it into a reliable physical product.**

# 4. Service 1 — Consultation

## Product Name

### Product & Technology Consultation

**Promise:**

> **Know what to build before spending heavily on development.**

### Problem solved

A company has an idea, business requirement, prototype, or technology ambition but lacks enough technical and product knowledge to make a confident investment decision.

Typical situations:

- “We have an IoT product idea, but don't know where to start.”
- “Should this product use IoT, AI, or both?”
- “Our prototype works, but is the architecture appropriate?”
- “How much will this product cost to develop?”
- “What hardware and software should we use?”
- “Should we build or buy part of the system?”
- “Is this product technically and commercially realistic?”

### What POGOT offers

POGOT provides structured analysis covering the areas relevant to the customer's decision:

**Business / Product**
- Problem and use-case definition
- Target customer and market-fit analysis
- Product concept validation
- MVP and feature prioritization
- Build-vs-buy considerations

**Technical**
- Technical feasibility
- Product and system architecture
- Hardware / MCU / SoC selection
- Firmware and embedded architecture
- Connectivity and IoT architecture
- Edge / cloud architecture
- AI / AIoT feasibility
- Technical risks and dependencies

**Financial / Planning**
- Development budget estimate
- Prototype budget
- Preliminary BOM / unit-cost estimate
- Development effort estimate
- Production considerations

### Consultation Deliverable

The customer receives a documented decision package, typically containing:

```text
Problem Definition
      ↓
Product Concept
      ↓
Feasibility Assessment
      ↓
Recommended Architecture
      ↓
Estimated Cost / Budget
      ↓
Technical Risks
      ↓
Recommended Implementation Scope
```

### Consultation packages

| Package | Purpose | Main outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Idea Validation** | Evaluate a new product concept | Go / revise / no-go recommendation |
| **Technical Feasibility** | Determine whether and how the product can be built | Feasibility + technical direction |
| **Product Architecture** | Design the technical system | Architecture + component decisions |
| **Product Planning** | Estimate scope, budget and technical execution | Development plan + cost assumptions |
| **Prototype Assessment** | Review an existing prototype | Risks, gaps and recommended next actions |

Packages can be combined when a customer needs a deeper engagement.

# 5. Service 2 — Product Development

## Product Name

### Embedded, IoT & AIoT Product Development

**Promise:**

> **Turn a concept or prototype into a working product.**

### Problem solved

A company knows what it wants to build but does not have the internal engineering capability, resources, or cross-disciplinary expertise to deliver the product.

Typical situations:

- “We need a working prototype.”
- “Our hardware needs firmware.”
- “We need to connect our machine to the cloud.”
- “We need an embedded Linux gateway.”
- “Our prototype works but is not production-ready.”
- “We need hardware, firmware and IoT developed together.”
- “We want to add AI/AIoT capabilities to our physical product.”

### What POGOT offers

POGOT can provide only the parts the customer needs or take responsibility for the complete product stack.

```text
Hardware
   ↓
Embedded Firmware
   ↓
Connectivity
   ↓
Edge / Gateway
   ↓
IoT Backend
   ↓
AI / AIoT
   ↓
Application / Integration
```

### Engineering capabilities

**Hardware**
- Embedded hardware architecture
- MCU / SoC selection
- Sensor and actuator integration
- Communication interfaces
- Prototype electronics integration

**Firmware**
- C / C++ firmware
- RTOS-based systems
- Drivers and peripheral integration
- Device state machines
- Power management
- Watchdogs and fault recovery
- Connectivity and protocol implementation
- OTA and device lifecycle management

**Embedded Linux / Edge**
- Linux-based gateways
- Buildroot / Yocto-based systems
- Hardware bring-up
- Device interfaces
- Local processing
- Edge connectivity

**IoT Systems**
- MQTT / device communication
- Device identity and provisioning
- Telemetry ingestion
- Backend services
- APIs
- Data storage
- Device monitoring

**AI / AIoT**
- Sensor-data intelligence
- Edge inference where appropriate
- Rules and anomaly detection
- AI-enabled product features
- AIoT system architecture

**Production Engineering**
- Firmware hardening
- Recovery / failure handling
- OTA and rollback
- Manufacturing test support
- Production configuration
- Deployment documentation

### Product Development packages

| Package | Purpose | Main outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Proof of Concept** | Validate a critical technical assumption | Working technical proof |
| **MVP Development** | Build the first usable version | Functional product prototype |
| **Complete Product Development** | Develop the required hardware/software system | Integrated product |
| **Productionization** | Move a prototype toward real deployment | More reliable, maintainable and production-ready product |
| **Product Evolution** | Improve an existing product | New features, revisions, fixes and lifecycle engineering |

The exact combination of hardware, firmware, IoT, edge and AIoT is determined by the customer's product requirements rather than by a fixed technology stack.

# 6. Core Differentiation

POGOT STUDIO differentiates itself through the intersection of:

```text
Business Understanding
        +
Product Strategy
        +
Embedded Engineering
        +
IoT / Edge Engineering
        +
AI / AIoT when justified
        +
Production Thinking
```

The company does **not** sell technology because it is fashionable.

### Principle

> **Start with the business problem. Select the minimum appropriate technology. Build the product around the requirement.**

For example, a customer asking for an “AIoT system” may actually need:

```text
Instrumentation
      ↓
Reliable data collection
      ↓
Connectivity
      ↓
Operational rules
      ↓
Historical data
      ↓
AI / ML when justified
```

POGOT is willing to recommend a simpler solution when a simpler solution provides better business value.

# 7. Target Customers

POGOT STUDIO is primarily aimed at B2B organizations that:

- operate or manufacture physical equipment;
- want to create a new connected product;
- have an embedded / IoT prototype;
- need digital technology integrated into physical operations;
- lack internal embedded engineering capability;
- need cross-disciplinary hardware and software development;
- need help evaluating technology investments.

Potential sectors include:

- Industrial and manufacturing
- Energy and renewable energy
- Water and utilities
- Agriculture and aquaculture
- Marine and maritime
- Logistics and fleet equipment
- Industrial automation
- Equipment manufacturers
- Technology startups developing physical products

The vertical is secondary to the core problem: **building or improving a connected physical product.**

# 9. What POGOT Does Not Promise

POGOT should not position itself as:

- a generic software outsourcing company;
- a pure IoT dashboard provider;
- an AI company that adds AI to everything;
- a hardware manufacturer for every product category;
- a management consultancy disconnected from engineering reality.

POGOT's value is the ability to connect **business requirements to real engineering execution**.

# 10. Long-Term Product Strategy

The immediate business is service-led, but services are not the final objective.

Every customer engagement should reveal repeated problems that could eventually become reusable technology or a standalone product.

```text
Customer Problem
      ↓
Consultation
      ↓
Product Development
      ↓
Repeated Engineering Problem
      ↓
Reusable Internal Capability
      ↓
Validated Product Opportunity
```

Possible future product areas may include:

- device management
- fleet/device diagnostics
- OTA infrastructure
- industrial edge gateways
- equipment reliability monitoring
- reusable embedded software platforms
- specialized AIoT solutions

However, POGOT should only productize capabilities supported by repeated customer demand and willingness to pay.

# 11. Core Business Principle

> **POGOT STUDIO does not sell technology trends. POGOT sells better product decisions and successful product development.**

The customer should leave a POGOT engagement knowing one of two things:

1. **What they should build and why**, or
2. **Having POGOT build it for them.**

That is the foundation of the POGOT STUDIO business.
