# What to prepare before sending your design to a contract manufacturer

When handing over a PCB design to a Contract Manufacturer (CM), missing details cause delays and costly component substitution errors.

## The Essential Package
1. **Gerber / ODB++ Files**
2. **Bill of Materials (BOM)** with MPNs and acceptable alternates
3. **Pick and Place / Centroid File**
4. **Fabrication & Assembly Drawings**
