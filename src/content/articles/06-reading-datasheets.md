# Reading a datasheet efficiently: what to look for first

Datasheets for complex microcontrollers or PMICs can span hundreds of pages.

## Efficient Reading Workflow
1. **Absolute Maximum Ratings:** Prevent silicon destruction during early testing.
2. **Pin Configuration & Alternative Functions:** Ensure multiplexed pins don't clash.
3. **Power Consumption Curves:** Validate battery calculations.
4. **Register Map / Timing Diagrams:** Reference during firmware driver development.
